# SouthBay420 walkthrough — 2 to 3 minutes

Record your screen and voice (e.g. Loom) using the demo shop, which has a month of sample sales and no real
customer data. Speak in your own words; the lines below are prompts, not a script to read.

## Setup (once)

In the `southbay420` folder: `npm run demo`. Then open three tabs: `http://localhost:8787` (shop),
`http://admin.localhost:8787` (console, password `demo-admin-password`), `http://pos.localhost:8787`
(register: Owner, same password, then PIN 1111).

## The walkthrough

1. **(15 s) Who and why.** "I'm Kyle. I've been in cannabis 15 years and I'm managing partner of Pacific
   Wellness. I built this with AI to solve problems at my own store." **[ask]** One sentence on the
   problem that started it.
2. **(40 s) The register.** Add three products; attach a member and show the points and "repeat last
   order". Point out the purchase-limit meter and what happens when a sale goes over California's daily
   limit. Take cash with the quick buttons.
3. **(30 s) The owner's console.** Home: today's sales, the typical sale time, what needs attention. Then End of day: takings by payment, drawer over/short.
4. **(25 s) The shop.** Delivery or pickup, filters, live stock, deals; then the same menu on a phone.
5. **(30 s) How I build it.** "I'm not a developer. I decide what to build and test every workflow;
   Claude writes the code inside guard rails I set up: automatic checks, and a second AI that reviews
   the first. Twenty-six releases, 168 automated tests."
6. **(10 s) Close.** What you want next, matched to who's watching.
