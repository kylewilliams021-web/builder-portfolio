Why I'm looking

I've worked for myself since my early twenties. I've built things that worked and things that didn't, and I know by now what I'm actually good at: finding the thing that's broken in a retail system and fixing it properly, then writing down why it broke.

At one store, that work stops at one store. I found five defects in our POS last month and had nowhere to send them. I'd rather do that work somewhere it reaches thousands of retailers than one — and I'd rather do it alongside people who are better than me at parts of it, which is not something a one-man operation can offer.
