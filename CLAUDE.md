# Working on this portfolio (for AI assistants)

This repository presents the owner as a **product builder**: a non-technical builder who ships real
products with AI, with taste, frameworks, curiosity and work ethic. Readers are hiring managers with two to
five minutes. Every page must be true, specific and quick to skim.

## Rules

- **Evidence over claims.** Every claim points to something checkable: a release, a test count, a decision
  record, a live site. Never inflate. If a fact is unknown, write **[ask]** and add it to
  `open-questions.md`.
- **The owner's reasoning is theirs.** Quote them, or write "AI recommended…; I agreed". Never invent their
  thinking or the frameworks they used.
- **Honest about AI.** The split (I decide, test and ship; the AI writes code inside a harness) is the
  point. Never hide it.
- **Privacy.** The business may be named. Business numbers (sales, members, orders) only once the owner
  has approved them: mark them **[OK?]** until then. Never include internal IDs (Cloudflare account,
  database), customer data, secrets or private repository internals beyond what a case study needs.
- **Plain words, first person,** short sentences. Technical depth lives in the project repositories; link
  to them.

## Working with the owner

17 years in cannabis; managing partner of Pacific Wellness; moving into tech (cannabis tech first, open to
anything). Private context: `private/job-search.md`. The work is "as much building, as it is learning by
doing, as it is showcasing", so:

- **Clarify exhaustively, up front:** every question whose answer would change the output, grouped,
  with options and a recommendation.
- **Ask before recommending** on big or taste-driven calls (positioning, wording, what to show to whom);
  small reversible calls: recommend, go ahead and say so.
- **Teach, tailored:** name the concept; one line if they know it, a few sentences if it's new, a
  concrete example if they're struggling.
- **Drafts only:** posts, messages and applications are drafted for the owner to edit and send. Never
  post or send anything for them.

## Structure

`README.md` (30-second front page) · `how-i-build.md` · `projects/<name>.md` (5-minute case studies) ·
`timeline.md` · `learning-log.md` · `open-questions.md` · `showcase/` (walkthrough script) · `posts/`
(build-in-public drafts) · `private/` (job search; deleted before going public).

## Keeping it current

Source material lives in each product repository: `docs/PRODUCT.md`, `docs/build-log/`,
`docs/product-decisions/`, `CHANGELOG.md`. At least monthly, curate new build-log entries into the case
studies, timeline and learning log. Interview the owner a few questions at a time from
`open-questions.md`; ask their opinion before giving yours, and teach the concept behind each question.
