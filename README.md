# Kyle Williams — Operator turned product builder

Seventeen years in California cannabis: wholesale brokering, then managing partner of Pacific Wellness,
a licensed dispensary where I make the day-to-day decisions. I signed on in December 2023, worked
through years of delays, took operational control in September 2025 and opened the doors in April 2026. I've wanted to move into tech since 2023; AI finally let me build, and I haven't stopped.

I find the problem, make the product calls, set the quality bar and test every workflow myself. Claude
writes the code inside a harness I designed, so I can trust it. I'm not a developer by training. In the AI
era, I don't have to be one to ship.

I know the customer of cannabis software because I am one: the counter, the compliance, the vendors, the
loyalty program, the POS.

## How I work, in 30 seconds

- **Problem first.** Each product started from a real pain point running Pacific Wellness.
- **Frameworks out loud.** Discovery and jobs to be done, prioritization, metrics, and working backwards
  from the release note. Each case study shows where I used them.
- **The AI in my own hands, with guard rails.** Hooks stop the AI from breaking what must never break, an
  automatic check runs before it may say "done", and a second AI reviews the first. When the reviewer
  finds the AI's mistakes, that's the system working. → [How I build](how-i-build.md)
- **Documented as I go.** A build log and decision records for every product, written while the work
  happens.
