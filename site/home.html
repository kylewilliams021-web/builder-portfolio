SouthBay420 · in development

Online ordering and a register for a licensed California dispensary

Menu and delivery, customer accounts with loyalty, a staff console and an in-store register. California rules are built into the sale: daily purchase limits with a meter that locks Charge, ID checks, edible potency caps. Metrc validated it as a third-party integrator and issued production keys.

No store runs on it yet, on purpose. It goes live after months in the Metrc sandbox without errors I can't fix, and once every feature is at least as good as what's on the market.

Canna Cash · my first product

Loyalty and referrals for Pacific Wellness, connected to Blaze

Built between August 6 and September 17, 2026. Balance search went from 2.7 seconds to 0.03 seconds per keystroke, at 19,500 transactions. It also taught me the lesson SouthBay420 is built on: loyalty that sits outside the point-of-sale is a pain to run.

Claude writes the code. I built the checks it has to pass.

I can't read the code, so on October 11 I built a harness around Claude. Code-level rules stop it from running commands against the live database or deploying without me, a check runs before it may say it's done, and a second AI reviews its work. On its first day the reviewer found 9 problems, then 6 more that the fixes had opened. Each one was fixed with a test.
