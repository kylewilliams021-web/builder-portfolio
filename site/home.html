Field notes from the counter

Running the store means running its software. These are problems I worked through on our point of sale, with the numbers.

  1. 465 SKUs repriced overnight. No receipt total moved.

    Out-the-door to pre-tax shelf prices in one night, with every customer paying the same total. Five POS defects the vendor doesn't document, and what I'd change in the product.

  2. Every brand test said the promotions worked. The store still lost money.

    A break-even for vendor discounts (F = d / (1 − d)), the brand test that passed, and the gross-profit-per-day chart that overturned it.
